# Makemories Privacy Notice

**Notice version:** 7

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

**Operator:** Lead The Way With AI LLC

**Privacy contact:** support@leadthewaywithai.com

Makemories helps caregivers and children make, learn and spend time together. Adults can collect ideas from supported websites and apps, or from photos they choose, turn them into craft instructions, and prepare for an activity together. This notice explains how we handle information when you use Makemories or contact us. “We” means Lead The Way With AI LLC.

Makemories is offered in the United States to adults aged 18 and over. Our [Terms of Use](https://www.leadthewaywithai.com/makemories/terms) also apply.

## Information we handle

**Your account.** When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple and our authentication provider, Google Firebase, process information needed to sign you in. We receive an account identifier and, when supplied, your email address, which may be an Apple private relay address. We do not receive your Apple password, and we do not keep the name Apple shares with us. Your crafts, imports and related records belong to your account.

**Your profile and children.** Both are optional. You can add a display name for yourself, which changes only how your name shows in Makemories; your Apple ID and email do not change. You can also add the children you make crafts with: a child’s first name, and optionally their birthday and interests. Makemories can also record which of these children made a craft. These details stay in your account. You can change them in Profile, and deleting your account erases them.

**Your account picture.** You can choose an optional profile photo. When you tap Save, Makemories uploads only a small cropped thumbnail and stores it privately with your account using Google Cloud and Firebase, so the same picture can appear on your other devices. The original photo is not uploaded. The app and server re-encode the thumbnail without location or other embedded metadata. Account pictures are not sent to AI or used to extract craft instructions. You can change or remove your picture in Profile.

**Your agreement.** We record which versions of the Terms of Use and this notice you agreed to, and when. This record is kept with your account.

**Craft ideas and saved content.** We process the links you submit, content retrieved from those links, photos you choose to import, generated craft instructions, your edits, titles, materials, tags, source references and retained craft images: a cover image from a link import, or a resized copy of the first photo from a photo import. Depending on the source, retrieval and extraction can involve text, images, video, audio and linked instructions. We store import status and information needed to retry or complete an import or save. When eligible, Makemories tries to save an import to your crafts automatically. We keep an unsaved draft after processing only if the import could not be saved. Your saved content also includes whether you marked a craft tried or a favorite, your optional private rating and note, and which craft sections you confirmed (materials, steps or both). In a saved craft, you confirm a section that needs checking by tapping “This list looks right” and saving, or by saving an edit to that section; an automatic save never confirms a section.

**Optional import-ready notifications.** If you choose to enable alerts on an iPhone, we use its notification address (a messaging token), an app installation identifier and protected proof, your account association, notification choice and registration versions to send alerts safely to that installation. The app keeps the installation identifier and proof in protected device-only storage, separate from your account session; this storage can survive signing out or reinstalling. We keep the current notification address with your account and limited server-only ownership records to prevent another account or installation using it. A fresh installation that has never opted in does not send a notification registration or request a messaging token for this feature. If this installation previously opted in, account switching or a lost local choice can send a disabled registration to revoke or replace that earlier association, even when the new account has not enabled alerts. Reinstalling does not automatically restore an enabled notification choice.

**Plans and materials.** We store crafts you add to your plan and the days you plan them for, materials you mark as needed, items you type into your materials list and their checked status. These records are associated with your account.

**Craft Cubbies.** We store the cubbies you create, their names, and which saved crafts you put in each one. These records are associated with your account.

**Information on your device.** Makemories keeps downloaded craft text, your Craft Cubbies, your plan and materials list, your profile and children’s details, account session information, a copy of your agreement to the Terms and this notice, and account-scoped import/save records on your device. Craft images are kept in a size-limited cache in the app’s temporary storage on your device, so they appear quickly and can be shown offline. The cache is kept between app launches and is cleared when you sign out or delete your account; your phone may also clear it to free up space. Account-picture thumbnails also use a small, size-limited cache for quick and offline display. The app clears that account’s cached picture and unfinished crops from this device when you sign out, switch accounts or delete the account. Temporary working files can remain after an interruption until the system clears them. Links that have not been accepted by our server can remain queued on your device until Makemories is open, signed in and online. Searching downloaded craft text happens on your device; we do not send those search terms to our server. Your Have checks and current instruction step are kept on this phone, separately for each account and craft, until you tap Finish or Start again, or delete the craft on this phone. They are not sent to our server.

**Technical and support information.** Our hosting and authentication providers process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps and error information to deliver and protect the service. Our application and request logs also contain identifiers linked to your account: your account identifier, craft and import-job identifiers, the identifiers of children you add, and storage locations that include your account identifier. Error records can include the host name of a source image that could not be copied, and an unexpected service error can record fragments of the affected record, which can include a submitted link or craft text. If you contact us, we receive your message, contact details and any information you choose to include.

Apart from the optional children’s details described above, please avoid including children’s names, faces, health information or other private family details in submitted links, photos or free-text fields. Because the first photo you import is kept as the craft’s cover, choose one without children’s faces. Source content can contain information about its creators or other people.

## How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, retrieve craft ideas, generate instructions, save and synchronize your crafts, maintain your plans and materials list, display and synchronize your chosen account picture, support offline reading, send the optional import-ready alerts you enable, respond to requests, tell you about important changes to Makemories, such as the end of the beta, troubleshoot failures and protect the service. We may also process information when required to comply with law or resolve a legal claim.

## Your agreement to AI processing

Before you use your account, Makemories asks you to agree to the Terms of Use and this notice. That agreement covers sending what you import for retrieval and AI processing as described below. Makemories does not ask again before each import, and there is no separate AI import setting.

- Nothing you import is sent until you have agreed.
- We record the versions you agreed to and the time with your account. Our server refuses imports from an account without a current agreement.
- To stop Makemories processing what you import, stop importing or delete your account under **Profile → Delete account**. Imports already started will finish unless you delete your account.
- When we make a substantive change to the Terms of Use or this notice, we increase its version and ask you to agree again before you continue using your account. Corrections of typing or formatting do not change the version.

## Optional import-ready alerts

Import-ready alerts are a separate, optional choice; agreeing to this notice does not turn them on. You can keep importing if you decline, iOS denies permission or registration fails. You can turn alerts off in **Profile → Import notifications** or manage system permission in iPhone Settings. Turning alerts off does not cancel an import or delete a saved craft.

An alert is eligible only for a new craft saved automatically from an import begun on that installation while its notification association is enabled. We do not send it to your other devices as a fallback or send a backlog when you enable alerts later. Imports that need manual resolution, edits and links to an existing saved craft do not produce these alerts. Native shared links accepted by our server can already save with the app closed; pasted links and photo imports still need the existing active-app save flow. Alerts do not change when those imports can save.

We deliver through **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** and **Apple Push Notification service (APNs)**. The visible alert is generic: “Saved and ready to make! ✂️” and “Your new craft is waiting for you.” Its routing data contains account, event, craft, installation and registration identifiers so a tap can open the right account’s craft. The notification payload does not contain your source link, craft title, photos or craft instructions. Google may process FCM information globally. Tapping an alert still requires the matching signed-in account and the app’s access checks; it does not grant access to someone else’s crafts. Notifications received while the app is open use a generic in-app message without automatically changing your screen.

Alerts can be delayed, missed or repeated. Provider acceptance does not confirm delivery to your phone. We limit retries, and do not automatically retry when an interrupted send may already have been accepted. Turning alerts off, signing out or deleting your account clears local delivery eligibility and tries to revoke the current server association while your account is still available; offline or interrupted cleanup may not reach the server. An alert already submitted to Google or Apple can still arrive. Fresh messaging-token collection and automatic remote-notification registration are disabled until you enable alerts, but the included messaging software can start up and process historical cached registration or incoming-message state after a previous opt-in. These controls do not promise that every provider request stops immediately or that provider-held records are immediately erased.

## What you import and where it goes

You can import links to Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube posts and videos and to public websites, by pasting them or sharing them from another app, and photos or screenshots you choose. Sharing a link from another app starts sending it to Makemories as soon as possible when you are signed in and connected. Once our server accepts it, retrieval, AI processing, eligible automatic saving and any confirmed cubby filing can continue without opening Makemories. A share still waiting offline or not yet accepted stays on this device and may need Makemories open, signed in and online to retry. Locally waiting shared links expire after 30 days and are removed when the app or share extension next processes the queue. For a craft-photo import, you choose photos or screenshots with the system photo picker, so Makemories never gets access to your whole photo library or camera; the app removes location and other embedded metadata and uploads the prepared images only when you tap Import. Account pictures follow the separate process under Your account picture. Only enabled sources can be imported, and a link from a source that is not enabled is refused before anything is retrieved.

When you import, what you chose is sent to Makemories’s servers on **Google Cloud**. Google Cloud and **Firebase** also run our sign-in, hosting, database and file storage. From our servers, your import goes to:

- **Google’s Gemini models on Vertex AI**, which read the source content (text, images, video and audio) or your photos and draft the craft instructions. We use Vertex AI’s global endpoint, so this processing may take place outside the United States. Under Google’s default settings, Google may cache model inputs in memory for up to 24 hours and may keep prompts flagged for possible abuse for up to 90 days. Generated instructions can be inaccurate; review them before using them.
- **Apify**, which retrieves public Instagram, Facebook and TikTok posts for us. We send Apify only the post’s link. For TikTok videos and Facebook Reels, Apify also stores a copy of the public video, which Makemories downloads. After each import we ask Apify to delete that retrieval’s stored results and files, and we retry a few times if deletion cannot be confirmed. Anything not deleted, and Apify’s own records of each retrieval, stay with Apify under its retention policy, and Apify may process them outside the United States. Photos are never sent to Apify.
- **YouTube API Services**, which tell us whether a YouTube video is public and eligible and give us its channel name, which we show to credit the creator. We send YouTube only the video’s identifier. Makemories stores the channel name with a YouTube import or saved craft and refreshes or removes it within 30 days. By using Makemories, you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google handles that request under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy).
- **Pinterest, source platforms and websites.** Our server retrieves Pinterest pins from Pinterest, downloads a post’s images and video from the platform that hosts them, and fetches the websites you import. When a pin or page links to its instructions, our server may also read that page and a few pages it links to, such as a printable, up to five pages per import; these can be on other websites. If our server cannot read a public page, it may ask Gemini’s URL-context tool to read it; Google may use its web index or fetch the live page. We do not use this to get past sign-in, membership, payment or email gates.

A public post can include its creator’s name, handle, image or voice; we use it to prepare your craft and credit the source. Importing does not give Makemories access to your social accounts, private messages, photo library or browsing history, and we never ask you to connect a social, YouTube or Google account. If you open an original source or print a linked printable, your phone contacts its website directly, and that website or app handles your visit under its own privacy notice. Please import only content you are entitled to use, and avoid private links or material that reveals someone’s sensitive information.

We share information only with the service providers named in this notice, and only as needed for their services. Google and Apify process it for us under data processing terms that limit its use to providing their services to us and require them to keep it secure and confidential, so it receives the same or equal protection as this notice describes. YouTube, Pinterest, the platforms that host a post’s images and video, and the websites our server reads receive only our server’s request for public content, not your account or device information.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Makemories contains no advertising or analytics software. Because we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, browser “Do Not Track” signals do not change how Makemories works. We do not allow other companies to collect information about your activity over time or across other apps and websites through Makemories.

We may disclose information where legally required, to respond to a lawful request, or when necessary to protect people and the security of the service. We limit such disclosures to what the circumstances require.

## Sharing

Your saved crafts and account picture are private to your account; Makemories does not publish them or offer a sharing feature. If you pass a craft or source link to someone yourself, deleting it in Makemories cannot recall their copy or remove the original creator’s post from its source platform.

## Keeping and deleting information

We retain account information and saved crafts while needed to provide your account and chosen features. Imports that could not be saved automatically remain listed in Recent Imports until you save or delete them. A link-import result expires after 30 days, as described below, so you may need to submit the link again to save it.

You can delete individual drafts and saved crafts using the app’s controls. Deleting a saved craft removes it from the library, plan and any Craft Cubbies, deletes its tried/favorite status, rating, note and section confirmations, and starts cleanup of its retained cover. Deleting a draft removes its draft text. Materials-list items added from a deleted craft are removed on a best-effort basis, and some can remain stored. Related source links, job records and limited deletion records can remain to prevent retries from restoring deleted content and to manage cleanup.

If copying a link import’s cover image fails, Makemories keeps a private retry record and tries again a few times within about 15 minutes. If you delete the craft or your account in the meantime, the copy is abandoned and never saved. A small empty placeholder file, which contains no image, can remain in our storage until automatic cleanup removes it.

**Link imports.** Temporary source media downloaded by our import service is removed when the import job ends. The generated result is kept for 30 days after the import finishes; the job record and its source link remain until you delete your account. Printable PDFs are linked, not copied. Images retained with saved crafts and information held by service providers follow their own lifecycle.

**Photo imports.** When a photo import finishes, we keep one resized copy of the first photo (first in the order you arranged before tapping Import, at most 1,600 pixels on its longest side) as the craft’s cover image. The uploaded photos themselves, including the full-size first photo, are deleted when the import finishes, fails or is discarded. Discarding the import also deletes its cover copy; once the craft is saved, the cover is deleted when you delete the craft or your account. Photos from an upload that is never completed are deleted about 15 minutes after the upload window closes. A safety rule deletes any submitted photo, and any cover copy not attached to a saved craft, left in storage after one day.

**Account pictures.** We keep the current thumbnail while you choose to use it. Replacing or removing it clears its active account reference and starts background deletion of the replaced or removed thumbnail and abandoned uploads. Cleanup runs in bounded batches and is retried after temporary failures; deletion may be delayed. Other devices stop showing a removed picture when they reconnect. Deleting your account includes these thumbnails. Deleted stored copies remain subject to Storage and backups below.

**Notification records.** A saved-craft notification event expires 24 hours after the save; the server stops sending it at expiry, and automatic cleanup removes it later rather than at an exact time. We retain enabled and disabled installation-association records while needed to enforce ownership and consent; they have no automatic expiry. When server revocation succeeds, turning alerts off or signing out removes the current notification address from that association. Account deletion removes the account’s registration and notification events and replaces its matching server-only installation binding with a limited protection record containing no account identifier or notification address. That record expires after 30 days and is automatically removed later. Deleted database records remain subject to Storage and backups below. The protected installation identity on the device can have a separate lifecycle from these server records.

**Notification providers.** Provider-held identifiers and delivery information have a separate lifecycle. Under [Firebase’s privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), Firebase keeps installation IDs used for FCM until a separate request to delete the ID, then removes them from live and backup systems within 180 days. Makemories’s current notification controls delete the messaging token and unregister remote messages; they do not request deletion of Firebase’s installation ID, so turning alerts off, signing out or deleting your Makemories account does not start that 180-day period. Google and Apple may retain technical and delivery information under their own policies; our event expiry and account-deletion controls do not establish when their copies are erased.

**Storage and backups.** Files we delete remain in our storage provider’s 7-day recovery period before they are permanently removed. Our database keeps a 7-day recovery window, so deleted records and earlier versions of changed records can remain recoverable for up to 7 days before they are permanently removed.

**Logs.** Application and request logs are kept for 30 days. They contain account-linked identifiers and are not erased when you delete your account. Google Cloud’s required audit logs, which record administrative activity on our systems, are kept for 400 days.

**Deleting your account.** You can delete your account in the app under **Profile → Delete account**; you need to be online. Deleting your account removes your saved crafts, Craft Cubbies, drafts, imports, plans and materials lists, your profile and children’s details, your stored files including uploaded craft photos, account-picture thumbnails and retained craft images, your agreement record, account-owned notification registrations and events, your sign-in account, and the Makemories data kept on the phone you delete from, including unopened shared links. Database records and stored files removed this way can remain recoverable for up to 7 days, as described under Storage and backups, before they are permanently removed. If you use Sign in with Apple, Makemories asks you to sign in with Apple again and revokes its Sign in with Apple authorization before anything is deleted. If the connection drops or the app closes during deletion, Makemories finishes removing this phone’s copy the next time it opens, and it removes only the deleted account’s data. A short deletion receipt is kept for 24 hours so an interrupted request can finish, and is then removed automatically, usually within a further day. Logs and copies held by service providers follow their own retention periods.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, delete your account or server-held information. Local copies and device backups can have a separate lifecycle from server data.

## Your choices and rights

You can enable or disable import-ready alerts independently of importing, choose which links and photos to import, choose, change or remove your account picture, and edit or remove supported saved content. Deleting your account withdraws your agreement to AI processing. You can contact support@leadthewaywithai.com to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request concerns your account.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a privacy regulator. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

## Children

Makemories supports activities that caregivers and children do together. Accounts are for adults aged 18 and over. Makemories is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The account and import workflow is designed for the caregiver; a child does not need a separate account to take part in the activity. You can choose to add a child’s first name, birthday and interests to your profile, as described under Your profile and children; none of these is needed to create a craft. We do not ask for a child’s school or health information.

Caregivers should avoid putting identifying information about children in imported links, photos, edits or support messages. If you believe we have received a child’s personal information inappropriately, contact support@leadthewaywithai.com so we can investigate and address it.

## Security and where information is processed

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider, and we use authenticated access and account access controls to protect it. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Makemories is offered only in the United States. Our application servers, database and file storage are in the United States. Some information may be processed or stored outside the United States: AI processing uses Google’s global endpoint, our logs use Google Cloud’s global logging location, and other providers, including Apify, Google’s messaging and sign-in services, may process information in other countries.
